# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are people evaluating Hridyansh Sandilya's work: collaborators, hiring teams, and curious visitors who want a memorable but truthful portfolio experience. They are often scanning quickly, while some will choose to explore.

## Product Purpose

Project Ouroboros is a first-person portfolio realm that makes a person's work, learning, leadership, and reflection explorable as a place. Success means a visitor can understand the work quickly in Classic View or discover it through a walk in the fog, without invented claims.

## Positioning

The portfolio's distinct mechanism is a navigable story-world: the visitor asks a question, follows pathways, and encounters evidence as districts rather than scrolling a standard résumé.

## Operating Context

The experience runs in a browser and should work offline after dependencies are installed. It supports a cinematic exploration mode, a fully equivalent Classic View, a client-side question ritual, procedural sound, and optional microphone reactivity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Drei, Framer Motion, Zustand, and Web Audio API.
- All 3D geometry, fog, and sound are procedural; no model, texture, or audio downloads.
- No personal state is persisted in localStorage or sessionStorage.
- Numeric statistics are not shown; scale and progress use words and named ranks.
- Unlaunched work and simulated demonstrations must be labelled honestly.

## Brand Commitments

The title is Project Ouroboros: The Grey Concourse. The voice is ceremonial, plain, warm, formal, lightly wry, and never pompous or hype-driven. The world uses grey fog, hidden councils, pathways, lanterns, and an original serpent-loop metaphor.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied project brief is the source for the portfolio facts, district descriptions, interaction concepts, and content constraints. No external proof assets are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the work discoverable without making it mysterious for mystery's sake.
- Let atmosphere invite attention, then let plain language earn trust.
- Offer a fast, equivalent route for visitors in a hurry.
- Treat curiosity, disruption, and rebuilding as connected parts of a story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, subtitles for guide dialogue, reduced-motion support, mobile controls, and an equivalent Classic View are required. The experience must remain usable without sound or microphone access.
