# Project Ouroboros design direction

## Thesis

The portfolio is a walkable instrument for asking better questions, not a conventional résumé wearing fantasy trim.

## Own-world

Cool grey atmosphere, ink-blue depth, oxidised bronze controls, and a living cyan signal. Surfaces are etched, thin, and softly illuminated; panels feel like field notes pinned inside a monumental hall.

## Story

The Wayfarer enters the fog, reads the founding myth, asks a question, follows a lit pathway, and leaves with a more complete understanding of the person behind the work.

## First viewport

A dark, fog-filled concourse opens immediately. The title occupies the left edge like a carved inscription; the council table and hovering sigil sit in the distance; the primary action is a low bronze "Step into the fog" control. A quiet Classic View escape sits opposite it.

## Form

Experience mode. The page refuses the category-default hero, feature grid, and résumé timeline. It stages the portfolio as a navigable ring with a persistent constellation control and a readable side panel when a district is opened.

## Visual system

- Background: ink `#0b1020`, fog `#8a8fa3`, deep violet `#26203f`.
- Signal: molten cyan `#73e6df`; ceremonial bronze `#b78d5a`; paper `#e8e1d2`.
- Display face: a high-contrast serif stack for titles; body: system sans for scannability.
- Motion: slow breathing, drifting fog, and deliberate panel reveals; reduced motion removes ornamental movement.
- Controls: compact, uppercase, keyboard-visible, with etched borders and no decorative glass cards.
